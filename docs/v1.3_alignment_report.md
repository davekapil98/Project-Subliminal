## Stage 1.1 and Stage 1.2 completed evidence

The first Priority-A source is Project IRA SO-101 at immutable Hugging Face
revision `bf6f568e9ec218fff838fb266e161724ec4f7e2c`. The CC-BY-SA-4.0
license, redistribution obligations, schema, exact source objects, native action
semantics, source-native units and limitations are pinned.

The qualification verifies all 844,208 numeric rows across 930 episodes and 93
prompts, plus 435,441,545 bytes of exact metadata, trajectory and episode-0
dual-camera media. Both H.264 streams decode at 30 fps, six real RGB frames are
represented in a canonical two-transition sample, and exact prompt groups are
kept wholly within a family-stratified 750/90/90 episode
train/validation/test split. See
`docs/stage1_1_project_ira_report.md` and the machine-readable qualification
record under `data/manifests/`.

ArmnetBench v0.1 SO-101 is pinned at immutable Hugging Face revision
`2e5e89aee0e7f081078d9d6ab3b279fc4b83ea84` under Apache-2.0. Qualification
verifies all 1,127,881 numeric rows across 2,499 episodes and all
success/failure/suboptimal labels. The bounded 664,617,401-byte subset contains
every trajectory shard plus one packed file per camera. Twenty-seven real AV1
RGB frames decode across the three outcome classes.

The source's stale aggregate-statistics counts and unreferenced packed-video
gaps are recorded explicitly; numeric evidence is recomputed from pinned
trajectories, explicit episode video timestamps are authoritative, and
referenced overlap is rejected. Exact task-policy cells remain wholly within a
deterministic 1,889/320/290 episode train/validation/test split. See
`docs/stage1_2_armnetbench_report.md` and its machine-readable qualification
record.

## Why Stage 1 is not yet complete

Source validation is not training admission, and two direct-embodiment sources
are not the full Priority-A inventory. Project IRA lacks published task-success
and quality labels; neither source publishes camera calibration or a common
task-space frame. Native actions therefore remain absolute source-calibrated
SO-101 positions, and no task-space conversion is allowed. The later
public-data gate must prove held-out target-model improvement without leakage or
unacceptable forgetting.
