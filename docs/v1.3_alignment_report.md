  active documentation.

  NVFP4 from the active baseline. Quantization is an optional later deployment

  pretending the tiny Stage 0 fixtures are full-size models.

  followed later by public and self-collected SO-101 specialization.

  units, frames, embodiment, revision, license, redistribution terms and
  checksums cannot be implicit.

  eight checkpoints and the exact v1.3 parameter budget.
- Preserved `.env`, credentials, raw/cleaned/normalized data, training shards,
  recordings, checkpoints and model binaries as Git-prohibited content while
  allowing only small reviewed manifest/split metadata.

## Stage 1.1 through Stage 1.4 completed evidence

The first Priority-A source is Project IRA SO-101 at immutable Hugging Face
revision `bf6f568e9ec218fff838fb266e161724ec4f7e2c`. The CC-BY-SA-4.0
license, redistribution obligations, schema, exact source objects, native action
semantics, source-native units and limitations are pinned.

The qualification verifies all 844,208 numeric rows across 930 episodes and 93
prompts, plus 435,441,545 bytes of exact metadata, trajectory and episode-0
dual-camera media. Both H.264 streams decode at 30 fps, six real RGB frames are
represented in a canonical two-transition sample, and exact prompt groups are
kept wholly within a family-stratified 750/90/90 episode
train/validation/test split. See
`docs/stage1_1_project_ira_report.md` and the machine-readable qualification
record under `data/manifests/`.

ArmnetBench v0.1 SO-101 is pinned at immutable Hugging Face revision
`2e5e89aee0e7f081078d9d6ab3b279fc4b83ea84` under Apache-2.0. Qualification
verifies all 1,127,881 numeric rows across 2,499 episodes and all
success/failure/suboptimal labels. The bounded 664,617,401-byte subset contains
every trajectory shard plus one packed file per camera. Twenty-seven real AV1
RGB frames decode across the three outcome classes.

The source's stale aggregate-statistics counts and unreferenced packed-video
gaps are recorded explicitly; numeric evidence is recomputed from pinned
trajectories, explicit episode video timestamps are authoritative, and
referenced overlap is rejected. Exact task-policy cells remain wholly within a
deterministic 1,889/320/290 episode train/validation/test split. See
`docs/stage1_2_armnetbench_report.md` and its machine-readable qualification
record.

SO101 MA MultiTask 700 is pinned at immutable Hugging Face revision
`d4ae15a1044198bced5b7401123888068033451b` under Apache-2.0, along with all
seven declared/resolved upstream IDs and revisions. Qualification verifies all
358,006 numeric rows across 700 episodes and a 567,845,886-byte metadata,
trajectory and representative-video subset. H.264 and AV1 decode, the
nonstandard task-text field is explicitly normalized, stale published counts
are rejected, and episode 447's nine unused frames per camera are kept outside
the aligned interval.

Contiguous ten-episode source-order blocks remain intact in a deterministic
560/70/70 episode train/validation/test split. A matched-update, three-seed
TinyBodyDynamics experiment improved held-out simulation error by a median
49.65%, but caused 12.90% median forgetting on Project IRA and therefore failed
the 10% public-data gate. See
`docs/stage1_3_so101_ma_multitask_report.md` and its machine-readable source,
split and value-gate records.

DROID raw v1.0.1 is pinned to exact GCS generations and collection-code commit
`33ae6a67274f36d2e29525b86f23a56616ef43a7` under CC BY 4.0. A complete
metadata namespace scan records 74,896 episodes across 13 labs, including
59,740 successes and 15,156 failures. The bounded 75,738,594-byte subset
contains one success/failure JSON-HDF5 pair per lab, all three IPRL camera roles
for both outcomes, and the official license marker.

All 26 HDF5 trajectories validate, published per-observation left-camera
extrinsics and original timestamps are preserved, six H.264 streams contain
exactly N-1 video frames, and first/middle/last decoding produces 18 hashed RGB
tensors. Raw identity fields are redacted, two expected stale post-hoc
controller labels are audited rather than made authoritative, and native 7D
Franka actions are not converted to SO-101. Complete labs remain disjoint in a
58,234/8,517/8,145 episode train/validation/test split. See
`docs/stage1_4_droid_raw_report.md`.

## Why Stage 1 is not yet complete

Source validation is not training admission, and the first controlled
admission attempt failed. Project IRA and SO101 MA lack published task-success
and quality labels; the simulation merge also omits its native-to-URDF
calibration. The three sources do not publish a common camera/task-space frame.
Native actions therefore remain absolute source-calibrated positions, and no
task-space conversion is allowed. A later gate must improve held-out targets
without leakage or unacceptable real-source forgetting.

after those sources are pinned, validated, canonicalized and reproducibly
